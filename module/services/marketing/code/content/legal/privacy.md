## Replace before launch

This is a development template, not legal advice and not a real company's privacy notice. The adopter must document its actual controller identity, purposes, legal bases, data categories, recipients, retention, international transfers, rights, contacts, and complaint process.

## Public starter behavior

The default marketing service loads no analytics, advertising, replay, or chat adapter. It exposes no contact form until a real communication contract is configured. Operational request logs must exclude raw emails, message content, tokens, signed URLs, and authentication data.

## Versioning

The revision in this page's publication metadata is the public version identifier. Authenticated acceptance belongs to the product's versioned consent record; changing this template must never silently reinterpret an existing acceptance.
