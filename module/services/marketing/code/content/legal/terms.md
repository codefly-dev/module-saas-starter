## Replace before launch

This is a development template, not legal advice and not an agreement offered by a real company. An adopter must review and replace it with terms appropriate to the product, customers, jurisdictions, billing model, acceptable-use policy, support commitments, liability allocation, and dispute process.

## Product authority

Plans, trials, taxes, limits, and checkout availability shown on the public site come from the product catalog. Legal copy must not redefine those fields.

## Versioning

The revision in this page's publication metadata is the public version identifier. Authenticated acceptance and consent evidence remain owned by the product contract.
